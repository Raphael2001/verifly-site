Verifly

Your email code, already filled in.

Verifly is a Chrome extension that finds the newest verification code in your open Gmail, Outlook or Yahoo Mail tab and offers it right at the login field.

Chrome Web Store coming soon

  • Gmail, Outlook on the web and Yahoo Mail
  • Emails in English and Hebrew, with more languages planned
  • Suggest or auto-fill, light or dark theme
  • Reads only the mail tabs you already have open. Nothing leaves your browser.
